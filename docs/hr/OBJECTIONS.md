# Objections: why a good teacher might not apply

Design input for the careers page (`hr/app/vacancies`), written with the
Hormozi objection method. The FAQ text lives in `hr/content/careers.ts`.
**Items marked (school to confirm) must be checked by the school before the
page goes live: nothing here invents salaries or benefits.**

| # | Surface objection | Hidden belief | Belief shift | Proof on the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "They will not read my application" | Private schools hire people they know | Every application is read and scored the same way | The process is shown in four steps; every applicant gets an email at each step |
| 2 | "I do not have SACE / BTPC / a permit yet" | I will be rejected automatically | You can still apply and tell us where you are | Compliance questions accept "applied for"; nothing is rejected automatically |
| 3 | "My English is not perfect" | The written questions will count against me | We mark what you know about teaching, not your grammar | Stated in the questions step and in the marking rules |
| 4 | "It will take too long" | A long form on my phone will lose my work | Start in a minute, save as you go, come back any time | "About 40 minutes", autosave, emailed link |
| 5 | "What will it pay?" | Private schools underpay | The vacancy says what it can; ask at interview | `salary_note` per vacancy (school to confirm wording) |
| 6 | "My referees will be bothered" | References are awkward and slow | Referees get one short form, about four minutes | Shown in the references step |
| 7 | "AI checks will wrongly flag me" | A machine decides | A person reviews anything flagged, and asks you about it | The integrity notice says exactly this |

Top three to address on the page: 1 (trust), 4 (effort/time), 2 (risk).
