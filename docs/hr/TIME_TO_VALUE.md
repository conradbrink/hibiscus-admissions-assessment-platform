# Time to value: the teacher application

Design input for the application form (`hr/app/apply`). Written with the
Hormozi value-accelerator method; every decision below is reflected in the UI.

## 1. Current time to value
- **Promised result:** an interview, then a job, at Hibiscus.
- **Full result:** weeks (closing date, references, shortlist, interview).
- **First win, before this design:** none until the whole form is sent, often
  45 to 90 minutes of work on a phone, with no proof anything was saved.
- **Key delays:** a long single form, uploads, written questions that need
  thought, finding referees' email addresses.

## 2. Friction map
| Step | Friction | Delay caused |
|---|---|---|
| Landing on the vacancy | Unclear what is needed before starting | Teacher leaves to "come back later" and does not |
| Long form | Fear of losing work on a weak connection | Abandons after the first error |
| Permission to teach | Unsure whether SACE/BTPC/permit is required | Stops to look up numbers |
| CV upload | File on another device | Stops |
| Written questions | Blank page, and worry about English | Longest pause; highest drop-off |
| Referees | Must contact referees first | Stops |

## 3. First win
**"Your application is started and saved"**, in under a minute: name, email,
consent. The email arrives with a personal link at once, so the teacher has
proof and a way back before they invest real effort.

## 4. Quick-win asset
- **Name:** the "Before you start" checklist on the vacancy page.
- **Format:** five ticks: CV as PDF or photo; teacher registration number; police clearance date if you have one; two referees' email addresses; about 40 minutes for the written questions.
- **What it does:** removes every mid-form stop by telling the teacher what to have ready.
- **Time to result:** 30 seconds to read.

## 5. Onboarding flow
- **Minute 0:** vacancy page, the checklist, one button: "Start my application" (three fields).
- **Minute 1:** the overview page with seven short sections, each with a time estimate, and "Saved" showing on every change. Email with the continue link already sent.
- **Day 1:** sections can be done in any order, across days and devices; the link brings them back to where they stopped.

## 6. Time reduction
- Ask only what the decision needs; nothing the school can collect after an offer (bank details, tax number, next of kin) is asked here.
- Autosave every answer as it is typed; never a "Save" button that can be forgotten.
- Sections are independent: references can be added last, after the teacher has spoken to their referees.

## 7. Effort reduction
- Plain English (B1 to B2) in every label; examples under the hard fields ("SACE number, e.g. 12345678").
- Yes/No buttons, not dropdowns, for declarations.
- A word counter and the word limit beside every written question, and a short "What we look for" line, so the blank page is not blank.
- Camera photos of certificates accepted, not only PDFs.

## 8. Perceived speed
- "About 40 minutes. You can stop and come back." on the vacancy page.
- Progress shown as "3 of 7 sections done", with each finished section ticked.
- Each section shows its own estimate ("2 minutes", "15 to 25 minutes").

## 9. Honesty about the AI check
Said once, plainly, before the questions, and ticked: we check every answer
for AI-written text; a flagged answer is discussed at interview, not rejected.
Framed as fairness to every teacher who writes their own answers.
